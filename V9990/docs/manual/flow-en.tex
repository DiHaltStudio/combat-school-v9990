\begin{enumerate}
\item The MSX screen shows \texttt{COMBAT SCHOOL}, \texttt{KONAMI 1987}, \texttt{DIHALT 2026} and the result of the V9990 check.
\item The DiHalt logo plays its fanfare for about eight seconds. The illustrated cover follows. Press and release any key to go on.
\item At \texttt{PRESS SPACE}, press \key{SPACE}, \key{RETURN} or joystick button~1. The introduction presents Nick, the player, and Joe, the computer-controlled rival.
\item Wait for the instructor's signal before starting the obstacle course. Between events you see your result and a map of your progress.
\end{enumerate}
After a failed event you may be offered a retry on the pull-up bar. If the run ends, the ranking appears and the game returns to the title. After graduation, read the briefing and start the rescue mission.
